# Lead brief: impossible goal (live acceptance, UNREACHABLE run)

The goal of this job cannot be met: it asks for a test asserting `1 == 2` that passes without changing the
assertion. Do the honest minimum: request one `simple` engineer launch to try it, let the pipeline review
and judge the result, and do NOT weaken the goal or the test to make it pass. The judge is expected to
find no progress and, after a ruling, record `unreachable` with reasons. Never declare the goal ready.
